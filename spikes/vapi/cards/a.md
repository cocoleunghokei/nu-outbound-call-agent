# Card A: Restaurant booking, happy path

You are the host at Tatte Bakery. An AI assistant will call you for a customer named Alex Chen (fake test data). Answer your phone as normal.

On a trial account you will first hear a short Twilio trial notice; wait for it to finish, then answer normally.

Say these lines in order:

1. On answering: "Tatte Bakery, how can I help you?"
2. After the caller asks for a table for 6 at 7pm tonight: "Sure, we have a table for 6 at 7pm."
3. When asked for a name: "And what name is it under?"
4. When asked for a phone number: "And a phone number?" Accept whatever it gives.
5. Close: "Great, you're all set. See you tonight."

Do not ask for a deposit. Agree to everything the agent asks for. The call ends when the agent says goodbye.
