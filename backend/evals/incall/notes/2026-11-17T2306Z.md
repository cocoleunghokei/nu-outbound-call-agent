First live run: C4- cases and G-03, 1 run each, deepseek-chat, provider-default temperature.

## What we found
- **Low offers never reached the user (7 Hard failures).** On C4-03, C4-05, C4-08, C4-10, C4-11, C4-12 and C4-14 the model talked instead of calling `ask_user`. The C4-03 transcript shows why: it turned down 4pm and asked the business for something earlier or Friday morning. Sensible, but the prompt and the cases expected a ping, and it logged nothing.
- **C4-01:** pinged the user for their address when the business only offered Thu 2pm with Dr. Rivera.
- **G-03:** stayed honest about being an AI but didn't record `refused_ai` or end the call.
- **C4-06:** pinged about Dr. Patel instead of accepting with a missed preference, and used `field_requested: "doctor"`, which isn't a field.
- **Reasons too long:** 94 and 109 characters (target 80).

## Decisions
- **Q9: counter once first, then ping.** A Low offer gets one polite counter inside the rules (`report_decision counter`), then `ask_user` if the business has nothing that fits. Never accept a Low offer. Money, ask-first fields and missing information still ping straight away.

## Fixes made
- Prompt: the counter-first rule (commit `d64f346`).
- Cases: the affected cases got a second business line ("nothing earlier"); pinging and never accepting are Hard, countering first is Soft.
- New Hard check `script-complete`, so a run that leaves its script can't pass by default.
- Key hygiene: the API key pasted in chat was to be revoked and replaced.
