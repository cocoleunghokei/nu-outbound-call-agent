First run with structured offers (branch `claude/structured-offers`): the agent writes down what it heard (`offer`: date, time, person), and Python scores it against the brief's rules before the gates decide. C4- and T-01, 3 runs each, temperature 0.2.

## What we found
- **Hard failures went up, but not because the model got worse.** All 22 Hard failures are in C4-01, C4-02, C4-15, C4-17 and T-01, and every one has an offer dated **2025** (83 times in the run). The brief only said "Thursday 19 November", so DeepSeek guessed the year.
- **The gates refused correct answers.** Python compared `2025-11-19` with the rule `2026-11-19` and said the offer broke "On Thursday 19 November". In C4-01 run 1 the agent first tried to **accept** Thu 2pm, which was right; the gate refused, so it countered. C4-15 (accept 14:30), C4-02, C4-17 and T-01 (take the Friday fallback) went the same way.
- **The Python comparisons themselves are right.** Replaying every recorded offer with the year fixed: Thu 2pm and 2:30pm fit; Fri 10am and 9:30 match the fallback; Thu 3pm breaks "before 3pm" (C4-05); Fri 2pm and noon are not "morning" (C4-04, C4-16); Dr. Patel fits (C4-06). These are the comparison mistakes structured offers were built to remove.
- **Steady:** C4-03 to C4-14 had no Hard failures, the same as the previous run. C4-04, C4-05 and C4-16 had none either, as predicted.
- **Remaining model problems, not caused by the year:** no tool call on C4-13 turn 2 (3 of 3) and 5 other times; C4-06 asks Alex instead of accepting Dr. Patel (3 of 3); C4-04, C4-05 and C4-16 ping Alex without countering first (Q9); the 80-character reason gate fires in most cases, each refusal costing an extra model round trip on the call.
- **Totals are not comparable** with earlier runs (17–21 script failures): more cases, 3 runs instead of 2, and new "offer written down as heard" checks.

## Fixes made
- `app/calls/scoring.py`: `fix_offer_year` moves the offer's date to the year closest to the brief's own dates before scoring (handles December to January). No clock, still pure.
- `app/calls/prompt.py`: a `<dates>` block lists the brief's rule dates with weekday and year ("Thursday 19 November 2026 (2026-11-19)"), and the offer instruction says to use that year.
- Regression tests in `tests/test_calls_scoring.py` and `tests/test_calls_prompt.py`.
